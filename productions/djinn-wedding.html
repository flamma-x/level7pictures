The story

A Kuwaiti folk legend turned horror story, part of the Monsters 2 anthology.

A 1990s folk singer is booked for a wealthy family's wedding and realises too late that the guests are djinn. Years later, she tells a journalist why she never sang again. Inspired by a story told in Kuwait.
