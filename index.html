Founder & Director

Said El
Marouk

“A director who learned to read a room before he learned to read a script.”

Born in Beirut in 1969 to deaf parents, Said El Marouk grew up fluent in a language made entirely of image and gesture. Three decades later, that is still the language he directs in.

He trained at the University Film Academy of Belgrade and in Düsseldorf, then spent the nineties in Germany as a first AD, editor and cameraman, and as a director and reporter for German and Arab broadcasters, from Cannes to the 1998 World Cup.

Back in Beirut from 2003, he directed more than seventy music videos for the biggest names in Arabic music, before moving into long form: his Egyptian feature debut, and a run of high-profile pan-Arab dramas.
